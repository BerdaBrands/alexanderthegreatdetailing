Privacy Policy

Alexander The Great Detailing • Effective Date: November 5, 2026

Alexander The Great Detailing respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you visit our website or request our services.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a quote, or schedule a detailing appointment, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service location or address
  • Vehicle make, model, and condition
  • Requested detailing services
  • Appointment information
  • Messages and customer communications
  • Payment and transaction details

Our website may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type, device information, and website usage data, depending on the technologies installed.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use customer information to:

  • Respond to questions and quote requests
  • Schedule and confirm appointments
  • Provide mobile detailing services
  • Process payments
  • Communicate about appointments and services
  • Respond to customer feedback
  • Improve our website and customer experience
  • Maintain business records
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell customer personal information.

3. Payment Processing

Payments may be processed through Square or other authorized payment providers.

Payment providers may collect and process payment information under their own privacy policies.

Alexander The Great Detailing does not intentionally store full credit card numbers on its website.

4. Information Sharing

We may share information with trusted service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our business, including website hosting, booking systems, and payment processing.

We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

We do not share customer information with unrelated third parties for their independent marketing purposes without appropriate authorization.

5. Website Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand visitor activity, and improve the customer experience.

The use of these technologies depends on the services and integrations installed on our website.

Visitors can manage certain cookie preferences through their browser settings.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no website or electronic communication system can guarantee complete security.

7. Data Retention

We retain customer information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations.

8. Your Privacy Choices

Customers may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable laws and legitimate business recordkeeping requirements.

Customers may also request that we stop sending optional promotional communications.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults arranging automotive detailing services.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

10. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including Google and Square.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those independent websites. Visitors should review their respective privacy policies.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our business operations, website technology, or applicable legal requirements change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
